# Oklahoma WIC (OSDH): eligibility, adjunctive eligibility, income guidelines, and the documents an applicant must bring
Sources fetched 2026-09-13

All quotes below are verbatim from Oklahoma State Department of Health (OSDH) pages and OSDH documents on `oklahoma.gov/health`. No third-party source is used anywhere in this file.

---

## 1. Categorical eligibility and adjunctive eligibility

Source: **WIC** — https://oklahoma.gov/health/services/children-family-health/wic.html

Eligible categories, quoted verbatim:

- "Women who are pregnant, breastfeeding, or have recently given birth"
- "Infants"
- "Children up to the age of five"

Income basis, quoted verbatim from the same page:

- "a family income within program limits"

That phrase links to the income guidelines PDF (see §3). **The 185% figure is not stated on this HTML page**; it appears only as the 1.85 multiplier footnote on the PDF, quoted in §3.

Adjunctive (automatic) eligibility, quoted verbatim from the same page:

> "If you receive Medicaid, SNAP or TANF, you are automatically eligible for WIC benefits."

Other links published on that page:

- Online prescreening tool: https://wic.fns.usda.gov/wps/pages/preScreenTool.xhtml (USDA FNS, linked by OSDH)
- WIC enrollment request tool: https://osdhcfhs.az1.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_b8vNxstcQGy9YzA?Source=Website
- Oklahoma WIC Clinic Locations: https://oklahoma.gov/content/dam/ok/en/health/health2/aem-documents/family-health/wic/wic-clinic-sites.pdf

Also verbatim from that page, on the enrollment tool:

> "you will also be given the option to upload each applicant's proofs of identification, residency, and income, which are required for program participation."

## 2. Documents to bring to a WIC appointment

Source: **"WIC Reminder!!!"**, Oklahoma State Department of Health, WIC Service, **ODH No. P–692 (Revised 1/30/09)**, page marker K–43 —
https://oklahoma.gov/content/dam/ok/en/health/health2/documents/wic-reminder-p-692-eands-rev-1-30-09-8-1-09-2009-6.pdf

Caveat carried with every quote in this section: this OSDH handout carries a **revision date of 1/30/09**. It is the only OSDH-published itemized document list reachable on 2026-09-13 (see §4 for the pages that were not reachable). Treat the *categories* (identification, home address, income, immunization) as current — they match the 2026 WIC page wording in §1 — and treat individual accepted-document examples as of that revision date.

Opening statement, verbatim:

> "U.S. citizenship/legal alien status is not a requirement for WIC Services. The Oklahoma WIC Program requires that each applicant, participant, parent, guardian, or caretaker must provide identification, proof of home address, and proof of income in order to receive WIC benefits. Listed below are examples of documents that will be considered acceptable proof:"

### Identification (verbatim list)

- "Immunization record"
- "Hospital record (crib card, ID bracelet, etc.)"
- "Birth certificate"
- "Foster care placement papers"
- "Social Security Card"
- "Driver's license/Photo ID"
- "Recent pay stub (30 days or less)"
- "Medicaid document showing current eligibility"
- "Temporary Assistance for Needy Families (TANF) document showing current eligibility"
- "SNAP/food stamp document showing current eligibility"
- "Military ID"
- "Voter registration card"
- "Work or school ID"
- "Passport"
- "WIC Proof of Identification Form, ODH Form No. 225"
- "Court-issued document (guardianship papers, etc.)"

### Home Address (verbatim list)

Section note, verbatim: "(Document **must** contain the participant's home address.)"

- "Recent utility bill (tele., water, gas, elec., or cable)"
- "Recent pay stub (30 days or less)"
- "Rent or mortgage receipts for lodging/housing"
- "Medicaid document showing current eligibility"
- "Temporary Assistance for Needy Families (TANF) document showing current eligibility"
- "SNAP/food stamp document showing current eligibility"
- "Recent Leave and Earnings Statement (LES) for military personnel"
- "Property tax receipt"
- "Shelter verification"
- "WIC Proof of Home Address Form, ODH Form No. 373"
- "Foster care placement papers"

### Income (verbatim list)

- "Medicaid letter showing current eligibility"
- "Temporary Assistance for Needy Families (TANF) letter showing current eligibility"
- "SNAP/food stamp letter showing current eligibility"
- "Current pay stubs (two from last 90 days per person)"
- "Recent Leave and Earnings Statement (LES) (for military personnel)"
- "Foster care placement papers"
- "Unemployment compensation letter/notice"
- "Scholarship letter"
- "Retirement, pensions, annuities records"
- "Child support payment"
- "Disability assistance (SSI) document showing current eligibility"
- "Divorce decree"
- "1040 Farm or Non-Farm/Self Employment"
- "WIC Alternative Assistance Documentation Form, ODH Form No. 350"
- "Written statement from family/friends who financially support applicant/participant"
- "Signed letter from employer indicating gross earnings"
- "*W–2 forms or income tax return (most recent)"
- "*State income tax form (most recent)"
- "*Recent bank statement"

Footnote to the starred items, verbatim:

> "*Not acceptable for applicants who are employed. If applicant is employed or the family has sources of income not reflected on the most recent W–2 or income tax return, he/she must provide proof of income by providing a copy of his/her most recent paycheck stubs and all other sources of current income, as WIC considers the **current** rate of income when determining income eligibility."

### Immunization (verbatim)

> "Please bring infant's/child's immunization record to scheduled certification visit."

### What happens if proofs are missing (verbatim)

> "During your certification/recertification appointment, you will be issued one month of food instruments and/or cash value benefits until proof of identification, home address, and income are provided. Remaining food instruments and/or cash value benefits will not be issued until proof of identification, home address, and income are provided."

> "Call this clinic if you need help deciding what to bring or if you cannot provide any of the above information."

> "This institution is an equal opportunity provider."

A Spanish version, "¡¡¡WIC Recordatorio!!!" (page marker K–43a), is page 2 of the same PDF. Its lead line, verbatim:

> "Necesitará traer comprobantes de domicilio, ingresos, y identificación para su cita de certificación de WIC."
