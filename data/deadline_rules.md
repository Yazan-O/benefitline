Route note. The OKDHS policy pages at `oklahoma.gov/okdhs/library/policy/current/...` now return HTTP 301 to `https://rules.ok.gov/home` (verified with curl on 2026-09-13), so the current OAC text was read from the official Oklahoma Administrative Rules system that `rules.ok.gov` runs on: its API host `prod-ok-rules-api.tecuity.com`, configured inside the `rules.ok.gov` application bundle (`https://rules.ok.gov/static/js/main.d7c870bd.js`, `adminRulesApiUrl`). The rules.ok.gov browse root is `https://rules.ok.gov/code`; it has no per-section deep link, so the source URLs below are the API URLs actually opened. Section numbers were confirmed current by listing the subchapter from that same API. All three routes named for OAC text were tried on 2026-09-13: `policies.okdhs.org` does not resolve (DNS ENOTFOUND); `oklahoma.gov/okdhs/library/policy/current/...` returns 301 to rules.ok.gov; and the Secretary of State route on `oklahoma.gov` returns HTTP 404 at `https://oklahoma.gov/sos.html`, `https://oklahoma.gov/sos/oar.html` and `https://oklahoma.gov/sos/oar/oklahoma-administrative-code.html` (curl, 2026-09-13), and `www.sos.ok.gov/oar/codemaster.aspx` returns HTTP 401. ## Oklahoma SNAP recertification / renewal timing (OAC 340:50-9-6, 340:50-3-2, 340:50-11-1)

SNAP | renewal filed timely (certification period of 3 or more months) | File on or before the 15th day of the LAST MONTH of the certification period; the worker then certifies or denies before the period ends | https://prod-ok-rules-api.tecuity.com/GetSegmentsBySubChapterNum?titleNum=340&chapterNum=50&subChapterNum=9 | "(B) A household with a prior certification period of three or more months, who applies on or before the 15th day of the last month of the certification period is considered timely. When the household meets all requirements, the worker certifies or denies the application prior to the end of the certification period."
SNAP | renewal filed timely (certification period under 3 months) | File by the 15th day of the last month; participation no later than 30 calendar days after benefits were last received | https://prod-ok-rules-api.tecuity.com/GetSegmentsBySubChapterNum?titleNum=340&chapterNum=50&subChapterNum=9 | "(A) A household with a prior certification period of fewer than three months who applies by the 15th day of the last month of the certification period is provided the opportunity to participate, when eligible, no later than 30-calendar days after the date the household last received its food benefits."
SNAP | uninterrupted benefits after a timely renewal | Benefits available by the household's normal issuance date in the month after the period ends | https://prod-ok-rules-api.tecuity.com/GetSegmentsBySubChapterNum?titleNum=340&chapterNum=50&subChapterNum=9 | "(i) Any eligible household who renews SNAP food benefits timely is provided an opportunity to participate by their normal issuance date in the month following the end of the current certification period."
SNAP | what the household must still do to keep uninterrupted benefits | Complete the interview when required and provide verification due on or after the timely-filing deadline | https://prod-ok-rules-api.tecuity.com/GetSegmentsBySubChapterNum?titleNum=340&chapterNum=50&subChapterNum=9 | "(ii) To retain this right to uninterrupted benefits, the household must complete an interview, when required, and provide required verification due on or after the deadline for filing timely certification renewals."
SNAP | CAUTION on the "last day of the certification period" formulation | Oklahoma's timely-filing test is the 15th day of the last month, NOT the last day; the last-day rule in OAC 340:50-9-6(c)(3) governs only the issuance date of the next month's benefit | https://prod-ok-rules-api.tecuity.com/GetSegmentsBySubChapterNum?titleNum=340&chapterNum=50&subChapterNum=9 | "(3) A household applying for certification renewal in the last month of its certification period is not entitled to expedited services. When the certification renewal is certified on or before the last day of the previous certification period, the subsequent month's benefit issues on the second working day of the month."
SNAP | renewal filed late, without good cause | Loses uninterrupted benefits; worker then has 30 calendar days to certify or deny | https://prod-ok-rules-api.tecuity.com/GetSegmentsBySubChapterNum?titleNum=340&chapterNum=50&subChapterNum=9 | "(1) A household that submits an untimely certification renewal, without good cause, loses its right to uninterrupted benefits. When this occurs, the worker has 30-calendar days to certify or deny the application."
SNAP | renewal filed after the period ends | Counts as a renewal if received within 30 calendar days after the period ends; benefits are prorated | https://prod-ok-rules-api.tecuity.com/GetSegmentsBySubChapterNum?titleNum=340&chapterNum=50&subChapterNum=9 | "(2) Applications received within 30-calendar days after the certification period ends are certification renewals. SNAP | interview requirement at application and renewal (OAC 340:50-3-2) | Face-to-face or phone interview required before certification | https://prod-ok-rules-api.tecuity.com/GetSegmentsBySubChapterNum?titleNum=340&chapterNum=50&subChapterNum=3 | "(a) Interview requirement. All households initially applying for food benefits or completing a certification renewal must have a face-to-face or phone interview with a worker prior to certification, per Section 273.2(e) of Title 7 of the Code of Federal Regulations (7 C.F.R. § 273.2(e)) unless the household meets criteria in (a)(2) of this Section."
SNAP | interview waiver at renewal | Waivable when all adult members are elderly or disabled with no earned income | https://prod-ok-rules-api.tecuity.com/GetSegmentsBySubChapterNum?titleNum=340&chapterNum=50&subChapterNum=3 | "(2) Certification renewal interviews may be waived for households when all adult members are elderly or disabled and have no earned income."
SNAP | expedited service at renewal | A renewal filed before the certification period ends is not eligible for expedited service | https://prod-ok-rules-api.tecuity.com/GetSegmentsBySubChapterNum?titleNum=340&chapterNum=50&subChapterNum=11 | "(4) Certification renewals received before the end of the certification period are not eligible for expedited service."
