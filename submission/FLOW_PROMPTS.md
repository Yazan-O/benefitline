Constraints applied to both prompts below, so they can be pasted as they are:

- 8 seconds maximum per clip. Generate at the duration listed, or generate 8 and trim.
- Photoreal. No illustration, no animation, no stylised grade.
- No on-screen text, no signage, no logos, no brand marks, no readable documents, no visible
  dollar amounts, no visible screens with readable content.
- Oklahoma-plausible: ordinary Midwestern American people, clothing, rooms and buildings. No
  coastal skylines, no palm trees, no visible mountains.
- Faces are ordinary and unglamorous. No actors performing distress.
- Ambient sound only. The voice-over is recorded separately and laid over the cut.
